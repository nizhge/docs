# gtmbe-learning-loop


## Trigger
Daily after publication and weekly for performance review.

## Persistence
Load previous post IDs, publication receipts, analytics snapshots, UTM/campaign mappings and permitted lead data from the canonical records. Capture metric timestamp and data freshness. Never write invented figures or replace previous metrics silently.

## KPI tiers
1. Real business outcomes: attributable qualified inbound inquiries, booked meetings, qualified pipeline. Report availability/attribution limits.
2. Intent signals: relevant profile visits, link clicks, thoughtful replies, saves if exposed.
3. Distribution: impressions/views, likes, reposts, quotes and follower growth.
Avoid optimizing for raw generated post count or likes alone.

## Review method
Compare topic, angle, first line, container, source category and time, controlling for small samples and known platform data gaps. Distinguish directional hypotheses from statistically compelling evidence. Measure incrementality only when there is an appropriate experiment; don't claim causal lift from a simple before/after snapshot.

## Report
`published verified`, `drafts pending`, `performance windows`, `top 3 useful threads`, `qualified demand`, `lessons with evidence`, `one change for next week`, `blockers/next actions`. Keep links to original records; update content-planner with proven and disproven hypotheses. No silent modifications to campaign goals.
