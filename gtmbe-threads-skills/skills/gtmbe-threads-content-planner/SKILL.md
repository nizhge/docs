# gtmbe-threads-content-planner


## Trigger
Every week or before a campaign, launch, event or topical series.

## Initial content pillars (hypotheses, adjust with data)
- 30% go-to-market and B2B acquisition.
- 25% startup/product building and validation.
- 20% AI, intelligence and research workflows.
- 15% specific market-entry/VC case breakdowns.
- 10% founder observations and selective offers.
Use a balanced mix rather than mechanically meeting percentages in a 7-post week. Do not fabricate founder 'build in public' updates to fill slots.

## Plan
Use ranked verified candidate topics, ongoing GTMBE business goals, past 30-day archive, and accessible analytics. Default 1–2 potential daily post slots (first week test, not guaranteed optimal) and 2–4 carefully chosen manual comment opportunities when relevant. Plan 1 deeper thread weekly at most initially; test format after collecting performance data. Do not presume best times without account-specific evidence.

Every planned published post should have a subtle value-based CTA; avoid repeated identical asks or engagement bait.

## Output
Seven-day table: date/time Europe/Moscow, buyer segment, pillar, source ID, specific unique thesis, hook idea, container, owner-voice angle, goal, CTA if any, status, responsible reviewer. Include a 'why this week' note, source gaps, and 3 hypotheses to evaluate. Keep unscheduled fallback topics available.

## Safety
The content plan does not authorize publishing, sending emails/DMs, or auto-replying. Maintain continuity with existing logged items; no recycled topic without genuinely new material.
