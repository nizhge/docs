# gtmbe-threads-hook-extractor


## Trigger
When a successful post or profile is observed and a better hook structure is needed for GTMBE.

## Steps
Use an actually retrieved post and engagement numbers, or pasted text. If only a URL is available and text can't be accessed, explicitly request the text or skip. Identify the opening's structural mechanism (unexpected tradeoff, precise number, pain point, founder anecdote, named comparison, permission to question a cliché), single-vs-thread format, reply dynamics, and what makes the claim relevant to its audience.

## Attribution and originality
Store original URL, date, observed metrics and whether engagement is follower-normalized. Do not import source sentence, signature phrase, stylized cadence or unique example into GTMBE. Translate the pattern into an ABSTRACT TEMPLATE, then supply entirely distinct GTMBE concepts and evidence.

## Output
`source URL`, `structural pattern`, `confidence and missing metrics`, `why it might work`, `blank structure`, `two distinctly original GTMBE hook concepts`. Never assert a certain algorithmic outcome from one post.
