# gtmbe-threads-post-writer


## Trigger
There is a verified idea with a GTMBE original thesis to draft from; not for rewriting external articles. For **owner-owned** existing text use `gtmbe-threads-repurposer`.

## Inputs
Idea ID, verified facts ledger, original analysis, content pillar, audience segment, owner Voice Profile, publication-cleared Story Bank, intended outcome (`meaningful replies`, `saving/sharing`, `profile/lead interest`).

## Audience
Early-stage SaaS/AI founders, B2B teams, product leads, founders expanding to new markets, decision-makers wanting validated product and go-to-market choices. In Russian by default.

## Formats
- Counterintuitive but evidence-backed GTM opinion.
- One observed case with an actionable implication.
- GTM diagnosis: a mistake, its cost, and a better question.
- Short founder-led lessons ONLY from the cleared Story Bank.
- Market insight / simple framework / product teardown / before-after example.
- Follow-on connected posts for a genuinely multi-step explanation.

## Writing constraints
One useful idea per post. First line must be understandable standalone. Default 120–400 chars, HARD maximum 500 characters for a standard post (count actual Unicode characters, check account/platform capability). Do not assume a text attachment is automatically supported. No more than one hashtag, preferably none; very sparse emoji; preserve clear Russian spelling. No engagement bait such as 'agree?', forced outrage or fabricated drama. No automatic hard-sell. Include a context-appropriate, low-pressure CTA in every finished post (invitation to share a practical view, read a relevant resource, or continue a concrete conversation). It must not be meaningless engagement bait.

## Originality check
Do not paraphrase a non-owner source line-by-line. Include independently formulated reasoning or practical application; keep a source reference internally and attribution when necessary for fair context. A copy of a third-party hook is not originality.

## Output
Give 2 candidate hooks and one recommended final post (plus optional thread variant), exact char count per post, `what is newly added`, source ledger IDs, score, and a natural low-pressure CTA. Send to `gtmbe-threads-humanizer` and evidence audit; do not publish.
