# gtmbe-voice-profiler


## Trigger
Initial onboarding and weekly updates from owner writing, edits, or publications. This is a STYLE task, not a fact-generation task.

## Samples
Target 30–100 real owner-authored samples when accessible; accept a smaller sample with reduced confidence. Exclude quoted material, translated third-party writing, heavily AI-rewritten posts, and anonymous team work. Record each sample's origin and whether it represents a genuine voice.

## Profile extraction
Document Russian-language sentence rhythm, degree of directness, default punctuation, typical openings, use of analogies, reactions, questions, humor, personal/biz distinctions, CTA posture, multilingual code-switching, repeated terms and avoided tropes. Compare samples by context; a formal scientific paper should not override the conversational voice desired for Threads.

## Provisional preferences until confirmed by corpus
Russian first; founder/practitioner voice; concise, concrete, slightly informal, sometimes argumentative but not insulting; no gratuitous em dashes; avoid generic AI boilerplate, pompous startup language, excessive emoji, made-up vulnerability or first-person success claims. This is a **hypothesis**, not a completed voice fingerprint.

## Separation of concerns
`Voice Profile`: HOW Georgy writes, trained only on Georgy's own writing.
`Story Bank`: WHAT Georgy may assert, populated only with verified, publication-cleared projects, numbers, lessons and experiences. Store `approved/needs review/private` per item. Never leak private project, health, legal or financial details.

## Output
Versioned `voice_profile` with sample IDs, confidence, 4–8 anchored voice examples, 4 anti-examples, do/don't rules and a diff of what changed. Preserve owner corrections as stronger evidence than generic platform advice. Mark `filled: yes` only after reading authentic sources and confirming suitability.
